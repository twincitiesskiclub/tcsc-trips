Twin Cities Ski Club Assumption of Risk, Release of Liability, SMS Communication, and Photography Release

Read carefully before agreeing via checkbox.

1. Introduction. By checking the box below, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agree to all its terms. When you participate in any activities organized by the Twin Cities Ski Club ("TCSC"), you accept these terms fully.

2. Assumption of Risk. Participation in activities such as weightlifting, cross-country skiing, skijoring, running, trail running, roller skiing, paddling, cycling, mountain biking, ice biking, snow biking, inline skating, and similar (collectively, "Activities") involves inherent risks. These risks, which can be severe, include but are not limited to changing weather conditions, variations in terrain, equipment failures, collisions, and other hazards that could result in serious injury or death. By participating, you voluntarily accept and fully assume all such risks, both known and unknown.

3. Release of Liability. You hereby waive any right to bring claims against TCSC, its affiliates (including Three Rivers Parks), and their respective officers, directors, employees, volunteers, and agents (collectively, "Organizers") for any injuries, death, or damages suffered as a result of participating in the Activities. This waiver applies whether the injuries result from the negligence of the Organizers or otherwise and extends to all claims you may have.

4. Medical Issues and Emergency Response. In the event of an injury or health issue, while TCSC and its affiliates will endeavor to provide reasonable assistance, participants understand and acknowledge that medical assistance may not be readily available. Participants further agree that TCSC is not required to provide medical care or transportation. Should any medical services be provided or transportation occur, you agree to be financially responsible for any costs associated with such medical care and transportation. Additionally, you agree to indemnify and hold harmless TCSC for the outcomes of such decisions.

5. Indemnification. You agree to indemnify, defend, and hold harmless TCSC and its affiliates and Organizers from any claims, losses, damages, or expenses, including legal fees, arising from your actions or failures to act during the Activities. This duty to indemnify will continue beyond the duration of your participation.

6. Photography and Image Use. By participating, you grant TCSC the right to use your photographs and images for promotional purposes without compensation. These images may be used in various media formats, globally.

7. SMS Communication Consent. You consent to receive SMS messages from TCSC containing practice information, updates, and other team-related information. You can opt out of these communications at any time by emailing contact@twincitiesskiclub.org with your request.

8. Guest Participation and Liability.

A. Guest Waiver Agreement. Guests are permitted at TCSC events and must agree to the same terms outlined in this document. If you bring a guest to a TCSC event, you will ensure, and you represent and warrant, that each Guest you bring has read and agreed to this Release and Waiver, which shall apply to each of them as if the reference to "you" was a reference to them.

B. Responsibility for Guests. You are responsible for your guests' actions during Activities and agree to indemnify and hold harmless TCSC and the Organizers from any claims arising from your guests' conduct.

9. Governing Law and Jurisdiction. This agreement is governed by the laws of Minnesota. Any disputes will be resolved in state or federal courts located in Hennepin County, Minnesota, to which you consent to jurisdiction.

This Agreement shall be construed in accordance with, and governed by the substantive laws of, the State of Minnesota (for any injury), without reference to principles governing choice or conflicts of laws. In addition, you agree that all lawsuits for personal injury or related loss against TCSC must be maintained in state courts sitting in Hennepin County, Minnesota or federal district courts sitting in the District of Minnesota, and you consent and agree that jurisdiction and venue for such proceedings shall lie exclusively with such courts.

10. Severability. In the event any portion of this release is found to be unenforceable, the remaining terms shall be fully enforceable.

Acknowledgment. By checking the box below, you confirm that you have read this document in its entirety, understand its terms, and agree to be legally bound by them. You acknowledge that this agreement involves significant rights being waived, including the right to sue. You agree to this freely and without any inducement.
